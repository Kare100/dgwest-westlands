Sports Road · Westlands · Nairobi

Twenty-five floors above Westlands.

DG West is a residential and hotel-apartment tower by Reportage Group: 200 homes, from 41 m² studios to 90 m² two-bedrooms, with an infinity pool, gym and spa on the 25th floor.

Residences · floors 5–24

Three types of home, ten per floor.

Every residential floor has the same layout: four studios, four one-bedrooms and two two-bedrooms, arranged in two mirrored wings around a lift core. The areas below are the developer's published figures.

Floor plans

Drawn to the room sizes in the brochure.

These plans are redrawn for this guide from the room dimensions printed on the developer's 5th–24th floor plans. Hover or tap a room for its size. The brochure itself notes its drawings are approximate and not to scale.

Amenities

A rooftop floor for residents.

The 25th-floor plan puts the open-air terrace on the south side and the enclosed rooms on the north side. A yoga garden sits on the roof above. Floors 3 and 4 hold the shared facilities below.

25th floor · open terrace

  • Infinity pool, about 11.8 × 3.8 m (measured off the plan)
  • Baby pool and jacuzzi
  • Bar area and terrace with built-in seating

25th floor · enclosed

  • Gym and Zumba studio
  • Women's and gents' spas, each with a massage room, steam room and sauna
  • Lockers and washrooms

Roof

  • Yoga garden with planters

Floors 3–4

  • Restaurant and residents' lounge
  • Co-working space and conference rooms
  • Salon and barber, pet day care, children's day care and play area

Building

  • 24-hour concierge and reception, valet parking
  • Retail and convenience store in the tower
  • Fibre internet, double-glazed windows, built-in wardrobes

Location

Sports Road, Westlands.

Westlands is Nairobi's business and nightlife district, with embassies, international organisations and malls nearby. Drive times are the developer's own estimates.
